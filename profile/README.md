# Welcome to CoLab! 👋

We are the **Computational Omics Laboratory (CoLab)**, a research group led by **[Zhijian Li](https://colabomics.org/)** at the **School of Medicine, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen)**.

Our mission is to **decode the language of life through computation**. We develop statistical and machine learning methods to understand genome regulation, cellular states, and disease mechanisms. By integrating single-cell, spatial, and multi-omics data, we aim to turn complex biological measurements into mechanistic insights and testable predictions.

## Research

Our work spans three connected areas:

- **Regulatory genomics** — Understanding how DNA sequence, chromatin accessibility, and regulatory elements control gene expression.
- **Multimodal cellular modeling** — Integrating single-cell and spatial omics to characterize cellular identities, states, and interactions.
- **Predictive models of cellular systems** — Building computational models to predict perturbation responses and inform experimental design.

We are especially interested in applying these approaches to understand human diseases, including fibrosis.
